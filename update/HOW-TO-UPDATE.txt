YOUR CUSTOMIZATION IS 100% SAFE!
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Updating to v0.2.0 will NOT remove or reset:
  * Your profile, display name, @handle, bio, banners, or roles
  * Your added games, covers, or playtime stats
  * Your ps3_pic, ps3_video, and ps3_audio custom media folders

WHICH FILE TO RUN:
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. If you installed GLauncher with the Setup installer:
   -> Close GLauncher, then run "GLauncher-Update-Setup-0.2.0.exe".
      It updates the app in-place while keeping all your data.

2. If you run GLauncher as a Portable .exe:
   -> Close GLauncher, and replace your old GLauncher .exe with
      "GLauncher-Update-Portable-0.2.0.exe" in the same folder.
