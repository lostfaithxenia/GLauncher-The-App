Welcome to GLauncher Alpha!

DON'T WORRY - YOUR GAMES ARE 100% SAFE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Installing or cleaning up old versions of this launcher will NEVER
touch, move, or delete your actual games, game saves, Steam/Roblox
files, or personal documents.


SIMPLE SETUP INSTRUCTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. (Optional) Remove any old version first:
   - Double-click "Uninstall-Previous-Versions.bat".
   - It will reassure you first and ask whether you want to:
       [1] Pick the old launcher folder yourself, OR
       [2] Let it automatically search for old launcher files.
   - Nothing is deleted until you see the exact list and press Y.

2. Install GLauncher Alpha (Fresh Setup):
   - Double-click "GLauncher-Alpha-Setup.exe" and follow the setup
     steps to install GLauncher Alpha with the new pretty CD icon.
   - (Or, if you prefer no installation, you can run
     "GLauncher-Alpha-Portable.exe" directly from anywhere.)

3. Updates:
   - Once your Remote Update Manifest URL is configured in Settings
     (or bundled in future builds), GLauncher automatically checks
     for updates on startup and every 10 minutes.

Enjoy GLauncher Alpha!
